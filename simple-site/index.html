Who we are

Real support, day by day

Mulligan Recovery Centers of Connecticut provides a safe and structured place for people who are ready to focus on recovery. Our residential program gives each person the time, support, and stability needed to reset, surrounded by caring professionals and a community of peers who understand the journey.

We believe lasting change happens in an environment grounded in respect, dignity, and hope. This is about more than stopping substance use. It is about rediscovering purpose, repairing relationships, and creating a future that feels meaningful and sustainable.

Who we serve

We serve adult men who are motivated to commit to meaningful change. Many arrive at a crossroads: tired of instability, ready for structure, and willing to do the work. Recovery here asks for honesty, accountability, and effort. In return, we provide structure, support, and a community that believes lasting change is possible.

Frequently asked questions

Who is the program for?

Adult men, 18 and older, who are ready to commit to recovery and benefit from a structured, supportive home while they build healthy routines.

How quickly will someone get back to me?

Most inquiries hear back the same business day. Final timing depends on paperwork and coverage requirements.

What does it cost?

We accept Connecticut Medicaid (HUSKY Health) and self-pay. Call us and we will help you understand your options.

Can families be involved?

Yes. With the resident's permission, family members can take part in communication and in planning for what comes after the program.

Can providers refer someone directly?

Yes. Providers can call us or email our referral team to start the conversation.

Should I share medical details online?

No. Please share only general contact information by email or web message. We will talk through anything personal by phone.

Residential program

Structure without overwhelm

Our residential program is a 24-hour staffed recovery home for adults who no longer need hospital or inpatient care but still benefit from daily support and accountability. The setting is small by design, so each resident gets individual attention while becoming part of a community focused on real change.

We are located in New Haven, minutes from downtown and Yale New Haven Hospital, and on a bus line with easy access to public transportation.

What to expect

  • 24/7 staff support
  • Group sessions
  • Individual counseling
  • Case management
  • Peer support
  • Life skills & job support
  • Planning for what comes next

Settling in

From day one, our team helps residents build stability and routine, with a clear plan forward, including practical needs like work, transportation, health care, and reconnecting with family.

Growing stronger

Residents take part in individual counseling, group sessions, and peer-led support, and meet regularly with their counselor to track progress. They build a sober network through local meetings and community connections.

Moving forward

As stability grows, residents prepare for their next step, whether that is outpatient services, recovery housing, or returning home with continued support.

Admissions

Is Mulligan the right fit?

Mulligan is not the right fit for everyone, and that is intentional. If you are not sure, call us. We will talk it through honestly and help point you toward the right level of support.

Admission requirements

  • Men age 18 or older
  • Active Connecticut Medicaid (HUSKY Health), or eligible for medical assistance
  • Complete a pre-screening and phone interview

We welcome people who

  • Have finished inpatient treatment and need a safe, structured, drug-free next step
  • Have lost housing or need a stable place to reset
  • Need to move away from an unsafe environment
  • Want to rebuild routines and prepare for independent living
  • Want a recovery community for accountability and support

How admissions works

1

Reach out

Call or message us with basic contact details. No private medical details needed.

2

Screening

We complete a short pre-screening and phone interview to make sure the program is a good fit.

3

Coverage & logistics

4

Move-in day

You get a warm welcome, an orientation, and an overview of the daily schedule.

Insurance & payment

Accessible care in Connecticut

We currently accept:

  • Connecticut Medicaid (HUSKY Health)
  • Self-pay

Not sure about your coverage or eligibility? Our team can walk you through your options and answer any questions.

For families & referral partners

You do not have to figure this out alone

If you are not sure what level of care makes sense, that is normal. We will help you think it through. Family involvement is encouraged when appropriate, and we welcome referrals from hospitals, treatment providers, courts, and loved ones.

Clear communication

With the resident's permission, we set up simple update plans so you know what to expect.

Practical guidance

We explain next steps in plain language and answer your questions directly.

Planning ahead

Before someone leaves our program, we work together on follow-up care and support at home.
